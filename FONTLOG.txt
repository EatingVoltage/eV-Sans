FONTLOG for eV Sans
===================

This file records the changes to eV Sans, as the SIL Open Font License recommends for every Modified Version.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

eV Sans is the typeface of eatingVoltage. It is a Modified Version of Jost* 3.7 by Owen Earl
(indestructible type*), https://github.com/indestructible-type/Jost, commit 35f141c.

It comes in three upright weights, Light (300), Regular (400) and Bold (700, drawn from Jost's 500), as OTF, TTF
and WOFF2.
Its characters are Latin for the European languages, figures, fractions, punctuation, currency signs and
Δ Ω µ π. Jost's Cyrillic and Greek letters are left out.

eV Sans has no Reserved Font Name.


ChangeLog
---------

2026-10-25 (eatingVoltage) eV Sans 1.003
  - Curves stay a full stroke wide where they meet a stem; Jost thins them there. In a b d g h m n p q r u,
    ŋ þ µ and their accented forms.
  - The ascenders of b d f h k l ß þ end on the cap height (they rose 80 units above it), and t, ƒ and the
    other small letters reaching above the x-height come down in proportion. The dots of i and j stay where
    they were, above the ascenders.
  - Δ (and ∆) is an equilateral triangle on the baseline, its apex pointed on the cap height, every side a
    stem thick.
  - ¶ has a face cut into its head: round eyes and a smile.
  - The two-storey a of ss01 is spaced like Jost's again: 1.002 gave it about 95 units too much on its right.

2026-10-23 (eatingVoltage) eV Sans 1.002
  - Bold is lighter: it is 1.001's Medium, unchanged in shape, spacing and kerning, now named Bold. 1.001's
    Bold was too heavy and is gone, and with it the Medium; eV Sans has three weights.

2026-10-22 (eatingVoltage) eV Sans 1.001
  - The hooks of g and j are longer: they run on past their lowest point and end cut square to the stroke.
  - J sits on the baseline (Jost's reaches below it), its hook as short as j's, and is spaced like a letter
    with a round side; Jost's kerning that kept g, j and q away from its hook is gone. In Ĳ the J moves off the I.
  - y's tail is straight again, its end cut square to the stroke.
  - j keeps its place and its dot is over its stem again: in 1.000 the shorter hook had moved the stem
    towards the letter before, and the dot only half as far.

2026-10-22 (eatingVoltage) eV Sans 1.000
  Each weight is made from the matching instance of Jost's variable font. Changes:
  - A has no crossbar (Λ), in every A with an accent too.
  - The points of A, M, N, V, W, v and w are cut flat on the baseline and the cap or x-height,
    3/4 of a stem wide, instead of running past the line to a sharp tip.
  - M has upright sides; its middle comes down to the baseline.
  - The x-height is 0.706 of the cap height (Jost: 0.657); the ascenders keep Jost's height.
  - The capitals' widths move halfway towards even proportions: E, F, L, P, B, R, T, X, K, J wider;
    O, Q, G, M, N, W narrower.
  - 10 units more space on each side of every character.
  - a is single-storey (Jost's alternate); the stylistic set ss01 gives the two-storey a.
  - The hooks of g and j end at their lowest point.
  - t ends in a foot curving to the right.
  - y's tail curves to the left like j's.
  - Every stroke is as thick as the capital stem: horizontals and the lowercase stems are made heavier.
  - Dots are square: on i and j, the umlauts and other accents, and in . : ; ! ?
  - Ω is as tall as the capitals; U+2126 (Ohm sign) and U+2206 (increment) are mapped to Ω and Δ.
  - Names, metadata and version are eV Sans's own.
